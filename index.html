Model and validation

The engine evaluates — candidate policies over six shared scenarios and selects one policy per component using dynamic programming. Its objective blends expected cost with the conditional cost of the adverse scenarios. The 95% tail cost is reported for the finished portfolio.

Company volumes, supplier terms, duty assumptions, and scenario probabilities are synthetic. The USITC records document tariff classifications and published schedule text. They do not set a buyer's effective duty.

Both baselines use the same capacity, budget, and recourse rules. The cheapest static plan chooses against unchanged assumed tariffs and normal demand, then gets scored under the same six scenarios as the recommended plan.
